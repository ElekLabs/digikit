# Ascend

A barber-pole phaser for Digitakt II and Digitone II. Its notches keep rising
or falling, so a sustained synth or loop can sound like it is climbing or
sinking without reaching an end.

Raise the Ascend send on a pad, hat or percussion loop to add motion to that
track. MIX controls the return level. With full send, MIX and DEPTH, the direct
and shifted signals can cancel at the moving notches. At zero DEPTH the return
is silent.

- **RATE** sets the speed from 0.04 to 0.30 Hz at full **DIR**.
- **DIR** sweeps down below zero and up above it. Move toward zero to slow the
  sweep or stop it at its current position.
- **DENS** brings the notches closer together at lower frequencies as you turn
  it up.
- **DEPTH** takes the phaser from the direct signal to the deepest notches.
